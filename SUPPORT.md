# Support DashboardDP

DashboardDP is free. The optional [Buy me a coffee button](https://ko-fi.com/K8E42B9WDB/?hidefeed=true&widget=true&embed=true&preview=true) does not unlock any features. In the extension, it is available under **Settings → About**. The donation panel contacts Ko-fi only when opened; DashboardDP does not receive payment details. See the [privacy policy](PRIVACIDAD.md).

Report ordinary bugs and suggestions through [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/PraenaDP/DashboardDP/issues). Use the [private reporting channel](https://github.com/PraenaDP/DashboardDP/security/advisories/new) for vulnerabilities. Never attach personal backups, passwords, or account tokens.
